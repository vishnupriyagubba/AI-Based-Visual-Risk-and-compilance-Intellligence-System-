How Visual Risk and AI Intelligence collects, uses, and protects your information.

Overview

Visual Risk and AI Intelligence ("we", "our", "the platform") is a personal decision-support application that tracks your finances, study habits, fitness, and goals, and uses analytics and AI to forecast outcomes. This policy explains what data we handle and the choices you have. By creating an account or using the platform, you agree to this policy.

Information we collect

We collect only the data needed to run the features you use:

CategoryWhat it includes
Account Username, email address, and a hashed password.
Profile Age, occupation, income, expenses, savings, and weekly study, fitness, and sleep figures.
Financial logs Transactions you add: date, category, amount, and type (income or expense).
Study & fitness logs Sessions you record: date, subject, hours, productivity score, activity, duration, and calories.
Goals Goal descriptions, target dates, and completion status.
Assistant & simulations Questions you ask the AI assistant, its responses, and the simulation scenarios you run.
Contact messages Name, email, subject, and message when you use the contact form.

How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account and keep you logged in.
  • To show dashboards, charts, streaks, and your Life Score.
  • To generate forecasts for savings, GPA, and fitness.
  • To run "what-if" simulations and store your simulation history.
  • To personalise answers from the AI assistant.
  • To reply to your contact requests and improve the platform.

AI processing

When you ask the AI assistant a question, we send your question together with a summary of your profile (for example age, occupation, income, expenses, savings, study and fitness totals, and active goals) to Google's Gemini service to generate a response. Your password and email are never included in that request.

AI responses are generated automatically and may contain mistakes. Please review the Terms of Service for the limits of this guidance.

Storage & security

  • Passwords are stored as salted hashes, never as plain text.
  • Your data is stored in the platform's database and is only shown to you after you log in.
  • Edit and delete actions check that a record belongs to your account.
  • Registration includes password-strength rules and bot protection.

No online system is completely secure. Please choose a strong, unique password and log out on shared devices.

Cookies & local storage

  • Session cookie: keeps you logged in and remembers your current assistant chat.
  • Theme preference: your light or dark mode choice is saved in your browser's local storage.

We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

Data sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information. Data is shared only with the AI provider described above to produce assistant responses, or when required by law. Platform administrators can view account and activity data for support and moderation.

Your rights

  • Access: view your data any time on your dashboard and log pages.
  • Correct: edit your transactions, study logs, and fitness logs.
  • Delete: remove individual records, or clear your simulation history.
  • Erase account: contact us to have your account and all related data deleted.

Data retention

We keep your data while your account is active. When you ask us to delete your account, we remove your profile and logs from the platform's database.

Policy changes

We may update this policy as the platform grows. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when it last changed, and continued use after an update means you accept the new version.

Contact us

Questions about this policy or your data? Reach us at support@digitaltwin.ai or use the contact form.
